# Cedar at the Ballpark

Cedar, an 85-lb grey brindle mutt, works the bleachers at a high-school baseball game for treats.

**Play it:** https://gregwerker.github.io/cedar-at-the-ballpark/

## How to play

- **Move:** arrow keys or WASD. On a phone, tap where Cedar should go.
- **Beg:** next to someone, hold Space (or the Sit & Beg button) until the puppy-eyes meter fills.
- **Who gives treats:** kids and grandparents are easy marks, parents are 50/50, and the team mom with the cooler has hot dogs. The 🚫 folks will call your human over.
- **Your human:** don't let her catch Cedar begging. Watch her line of sight (the yellow cone).
- **Umbrellas:** sun umbrellas are terrifying. Cedar won't go near an open one, and they pop open without warning.
- **Dropped snacks** on the ground are fair game. Baseballs are not: Cedar is a good boy.

One game is about 2½ minutes. Cedar's best score is remembered in your browser.

## How it's built

A single `index.html` with a 2D canvas game loop. Everything (Cedar, the diamond, the crowd) is drawn in code, and the sound effects are generated in the browser. There are no image or sound files. GitHub Pages serves the file, and the game runs entirely in the player's browser.
